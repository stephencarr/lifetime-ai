# G4 development run: local encoder and classifier baselines

**Recorded:** 2026-09-22
**Scope:** development partition only; no model predictions or scoring on holdout.
**Authorization:** `evaluation/config/g4-development-authorization.json`
**Cost comparison:** deferred as authorized.

## Current development protocol

The current result is `grouped-calibrated-results.json`, generated by
`scripts/run_g4_grouped_development.py`. It uses all 90 development families
(1,080 pair opportunities) in five outer whole-family folds. Within each
outer-training fold, four inner whole-family folds provide predictions for
temperature calibration and threshold selection. No evaluated family is used
to fit its model, temperature, or threshold. The local sentence encoder is
`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` at revision
`1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41`; it and the classifier run
locally, offline. The alternative is a TF-IDF logistic classifier. Both use
separate system-visible event/context and candidate projections plus their
pairwise features, not evaluator-only fields or opaque IDs. A predicted
`uncertain` relationship always escalates.

The sealed v21 corpus verified against declared manifest
`9052977faf208b1400c95fedf6aa4f0183a9d3dfc6ca92dfbaff6483a7c3afe3`.
The sealed evaluation policy and dependency lock were not changed.
Current development runners and scorer use a narrower manifest-pinned
development-partition integrity check: it verifies `development.jsonl` without
opening `holdout.jsonl`. This does not claim full sealed-corpus re-verification.

With thresholds tuned only on the inner folds, structured MiniLM accepted
790/1,080 development pairs (73.15% coverage), 757 correctly (95.82%
precision). Its ECE was 0.0198 and multiclass Brier score was 0.1546; the 33
wrong accepted decisions were 9 low and 24 medium severity, with none high.
Structured TF-IDF accepted 341/1,080 (31.57% coverage), 324 correctly
(95.01% precision), with ECE 0.0445, Brier 0.3274, and 17 wrong accepted
decisions (4 low, 13 medium, none high). Full confusion and per-class results
are in the JSON artifact.

At the **frozen 0.95 probability threshold**, MiniLM yielded 588 potential
candidate accepts (54.44% of development pairs), 578 correct (98.30%
precision; Wilson 95% interval 96.90–99.07%). Its ten errors were 1 low and
9 medium severity, with none high. TF-IDF yielded 303 potential accepts
(28.06%), 292 correct (96.37% precision), with 3 low and 8 medium errors.
These are **probability-gate-only figures**. The deterministic transition
validator was not applied, so they are not actual autonomous decisions or a
G4 pass. The fixed-threshold comparison is the relevant preliminary estimate
for the sealed policy; the tuned-threshold figures are diagnostic only.

## Earlier exploratory baseline

The first 72-train/18-validation-family experiment used a whole JSON feature
representation and evaluated 216 pairs. Both TF-IDF and MiniLM accepted only
2/216 (0.93% coverage), both correct. MiniLM ECE was 0.0477 and Brier 0.8065;
TF-IDF ECE was 0.1407 and Brier 0.4794. That result is superseded for model
selection by the structured, grouped protocol above; its artifacts remain as
development history. The intermediate uncalibrated grouped result is likewise
preserved but superseded by the calibrated result.

## Decision and limitations

`DEVELOPMENT_CANDIDATE_ONLY` — structured MiniLM is the stronger local
candidate for further G4 engineering. Its development probability-gate result
exceeds the provisional precision and coverage targets, but model selection
and feature/protocol work used development evidence, so the estimate may be
optimistic. G4 cannot pass without the deterministic transition validator,
unauthorized-mutation checks, real Baseline C call and latency comparisons,
and a separately authorized untouched holdout run. No cost claim is made.
The existing G2 `TransitionValidator` operates on G2 `Event`/`StateItem`
types and commits to the G2 store; the G4 corpus has a different event,
snapshot, authority, and state-effect contract. It cannot be counted as the
G4 validator by merely calling it on the relationship predictions. Next,
define and test a G4 transition proposal/validator over the system-visible
projection, then run the fixed 0.95 policy through that validator on
development before freezing an end-to-end candidate for any holdout request.

Initial scaffolding was added in `lifetime_ai/evaluation/g4_transition.py`:
an explicit effect proposal and read-only, fail-closed preflight that checks
identity, visible-source provenance, authority, and conservative proposal
shapes (`unrelated`/`none`, `supports`/`reinforce`, and explicit
`narrows_scope`/`add_scoped_item`). It intentionally does
not infer effects or test semantic correctness, and it cannot commit state.
A passing preflight is **not** a validated autonomous decision; the
grouped result above has not been recomputed or upgraded on its basis.
The preflight also requires candidate evidence to predate its snapshot and
reinforcement proposals to retain all candidate source-event IDs. These
provenance checks did not change the development diagnostic counts.
`prepare_g4_transition` initially performed a read-only preparation for the supported
shapes: no-op produces no state candidate; reinforcement copies the
immutable payload, verifies its canonical hash, retains counterevidence and
prior sources, and adds the current event as a new source. The scoped-item
branch requires an explicit new ID and schema-valid payload, strict scope
refinement with added keys and values lexically present in the current event,
verbatim proposition, compatible kind/validity/sensitivity, and source
authority; it prepares one source-linked snapshot while leaving the parent
unchanged. This lexical check is not a semantic entailment test. At that stage,
no payload extractor was connected; the result explicitly had no write
authority and was not counted as a G4 autonomous decision. The first
development preflight counts below were computed before the scoped-item and
replacement branches; later diagnostics and the current read-only rehearsal
are reported separately.

## Separate state-effect development diagnostic

`scripts/run_g4_effect_development.py` trains a separate MiniLM/logistic
state-effect head and a separate disposition head in the same five
whole-family outer folds, using only
system-visible pair text and the current event's two authority flags.
Their gold labels are independent from relationship labels and never enter the
features. It produced 988/1,080 correct out-of-fold effect predictions
(91.48%) and 952/1,080 correct disposition predictions (88.15%). Among the
existing 588 relationship-probability-gated pairs, 576 had both relationship
and effect correct (97.96%); 569 had all three targets correct (96.77%).
Effect and disposition confidence are uncalibrated. No state mutation was
constructed or committed. See `effect-development-results.json` and its
prediction artifact. The rare `expire` effect was correct on only 3/9
development cases, so the overall effect accuracy should not conceal that
weak slice.

`scripts/score_g4_preflight_development.py` then formed minimal explicit
proposals from those predictions and applied the read-only preflight. Only
138/588 probability-gated pairs passed its narrow permitted shapes; 130/138
had both labels correct. The other eight carry medium pair-severity tags.
Four of the 138 have gold disposition `abstain`, showing that this structural
preflight is not sufficient to authorize mutations. It accepts no actual
write and counts **zero autonomous G4 decisions**. The new diagnostic is in
`preflight-development-results.json`; it does not alter the earlier
relationship-only figures or create a gate result.
Filtering those 138 further to cases where the model-predicted disposition
matches the proposal shape leaves 133; only 127 have both relationship and
effect correct, and two still have gold disposition `abstain`. A predicted
disposition is not a permission grant.

The later `preflight-scoped-development-results.json` diagnostic adds a narrow
lexical proposal extractor for the current event's single `child workflow
'…'` phrase. It proposes a `condition` scope extension and copies the
source text verbatim into a new payload; it reads no evaluator field when
constructing the proposal. This development-tuned mapping raises read-only
preflight passes from 138 to 345 of the same 588 gated pairs. Of those 345,
337 have correct relationship and effect labels; the same eight wrong pair
predictions remain. All 207 newly passed scoped-item proposals have correct
relationship and effect labels in this development run. An immediate reference
snapshot exists for 152 of those 207, and the proposed canonical payload
matches all 152 exactly. The other 55 lack an immediate reference snapshot
in the development corpus and are not payload-verified by this comparison.
These are in-development diagnostics, not independent validation: the scope
key was corrected after comparing to development reference state, lexical
extraction is not semantic entailment, and neither preflight nor reference
matching grants write authority. Autonomous G4 decisions remain zero.

An error audit of those eight pairs found that every one is an
`unrelated`/`none` proposal: three are actually reinforcement, two conflict,
one replacement, and two require abstention. A structural no-op preflight
cannot establish unrelatedness, so the diagnostic now marks no-op proposals
as ineligible for an autonomy candidate slice without discarding them from
the audit. The remaining 291 state-changing, read-only preflight passes
(84 reinforcement and 207 scoped-item proposals) have 291 correct
relationship/effect pairs and 291 gold `accept` dispositions in development.
This is a development-selected slice and still lacks semantic effect
validation, a write-authorizing transition validator, persistence checks,
and independent holdout evidence. It is **not** 291 autonomous decisions.

A separate `witness_g4_transition` check now requires exact positive
source-language for reinforcement and a constrained old-setting/new-setting
exception statement for scoped changes. It passes 286 of the 291
state-changing preflight proposals: 84 reinforcement and 202 scoped items.
The five escalations use different, legitimate API-version or compiler
phrasings, so the narrow grammar is not stretched to accept them. All 286
passes have correct relationship/effect labels and gold `accept` dispositions
in development. Adversarial tests reject a negated confirmation, an unchanged
setting, and loss of the general rule. This is a development-tuned textual
witness, not a general entailment proof or a write-authorizing validator; no
new autonomous decisions are claimed.

## End-to-end read-only development rehearsal

`scripts/run_g4_transition_rehearsal_development.py` now replays the existing
out-of-fold relationship and effect artifacts through one explicit
development-candidate policy (`evaluation/config/g4-development-rehearsal-policy.json`).
It strips evaluator fields before decision-making, recomputes the frozen 0.95
single-class gate, excludes no-ops, requires predicted disposition `accept`,
runs structural preflight and the positive source witness, prepares a state
candidate, and checks payload hash, identity, sequence, provenance,
counterevidence, unchanged input projections, and zero write authority. Only
after all 1,080 decisions exist does the scorer read evaluator labels. The
complete pair audit is `rehearsal-development-audit.jsonl`; summary and
artifact/code hashes are in `rehearsal-development-results.json`.

The initial v0.1 rehearsal prepared 286/1,080 pairs (26.48%), below the
provisional 30% coverage gate. The current v0.2 development candidate adds a
strictly read-only `supersedes`/`close_and_replace` path: it checks an explicit
old-to-new activation witness, expires the parent at the effective time, and
prepares a new source-linked item. It admits 77 of 79 gated replacement
predictions; the other two escalate. Both prepared payloads match immediate
development reference snapshots in all 45 cases where both references exist;
32 accepted replacements lack that comparison.

All 1,080 development pairs meet the frozen eligible-decision definition.
The current rehearsal prepares 363/1,080 pairs (33.61%): 84 reinforcement,
202 scoped items, and 77 replacements. All 363 have correct joint
relationship/effect labels and gold `accept` dispositions in development.
The two-sided Wilson lower bound for observed joint precision is 98.95%;
all-eligible relationship ECE is 0.0198. This clears the provisional coverage,
point-precision, and ECE numbers **on development only**; it is not an
independent pass. The policy remains unfrozen and template-bound, no inference
was timed, no real baseline comparison was run, no mutation was committed,
and **zero autonomous decisions** are claimed. Cost comparison remains
deferred by the project owner. After this development rehearsal, an accidental
broad text search traversed holdout files. No holdout predictions or scoring
were run. The owner chose clean-agent quarantine for sealed v21 rather than
automatic replacement; any final evidence must disclose that it was not
literally never accessed. See `holdout-access-incident.md`.
