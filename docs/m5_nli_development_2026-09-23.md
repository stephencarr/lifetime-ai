# M5 independent local support model development — 23 September 2026

The earlier Qwen3-8B path used the same generator to propose and verify claims. It accepted a one-off episode and a gratitude message as durable state while sometimes rejecting direct preferences. This continuation tests a *different* local model for source entailment; it is synthetic development work, not the unseen independent participant preflight.

## Candidate and frozen instrument

The candidate is `MoritzLaurer/ModernBERT-base-zeroshot-v2.0`, revision `d421c4545a438fd006fb43f8b981c5d908faa1e1`, with its 151 MB int8 ONNX weights. The exact file hashes, local runtime, and tokenizer are in [`model_lock.json`](../evaluation/m5_nli_preflight/v0.1/model_lock.json). [`cases.json`](../evaluation/m5_nli_preflight/v0.1/cases.json) contains 48 source–hypothesis pairs: 24 supported and 24 unsupported, spanning paraphrase, actor, quotation, hypotheticals, uncertain commitment, dated episodes, scope, negation, prompt injection, routine facts, and requests. Cases, criteria, and model lock were hashed in [`freeze.json`](../evaluation/m5_nli_preflight/v0.1/freeze.json) before the first score. The predeclared rule accepts support at entailment probability ≥0.90 and requires at least 90% positive recall, zero false supports, and p95 inference under one second.

## First score and decision

The first retained score was **21/24 supported pairs accepted** and **1/24 unsupported pairs falsely accepted**, with 44/48 total correct and 0.0147-second p95 inference. The false support was a prompt-injection instruction containing the words `I prefer...`; the model treated that embedded assertion as evidence. It also missed three valid positive paraphrases or commitments. The complete per-item probabilities and labels are in [`attempt-01.json`](../experiments/m5_nli_preflight/attempt-01.json). **The candidate failed the frozen gate and has not been added as the product's final support authority.**

A separate diagnostic probe tested two generic durability hypotheses on nine frozen sources. The complete inputs and probabilities are retained in [`diagnostic-probes-01.json`](../experiments/m5_nli_preflight/diagnostic-probes-01.json). Under the `direct_durable` hypothesis, a quoted third-party preference scored 0.943 while a direct response-style request scored 0.009 and a definite Friday commitment 0.058. Adding `ongoing` reduced the quoted-source score, but also reduced a direct preference to 0.139 and the definite commitment to 0.016. The injected storage instruction still scored 0.490. These were exploratory probes after the frozen attempt, not a second scored gate. This generic NLI model cannot by itself decide whether a sentence is a genuine durable first-person assertion. Its useful role may be one signal within a separately validated speech-act and temporal gate, but integrating it now would risk replacing one unmeasured failure with another.

The first implementation lives in [`local_nli.py`](../lifetime_ai/local_nli.py) and the frozen scorer in [`m5_nli_preflight.py`](../lifetime_ai/m5_nli_preflight.py). Both operate entirely on local pinned files and synthetic inputs. Participant UAT remains paused. The live local POC database, private `.uat` data, quarantined G4 v21 holdout, and unseen independent sample were not used. Formal G5 is separate.
